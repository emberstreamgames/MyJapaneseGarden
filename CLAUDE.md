# My Japanese Garden

A Japanese study app whose chapters currently follow the Genki I textbook. There's flashcard practice for each deck, plus a garden game: plants grow when you finish 10-question quizzes. Everything runs in the browser and progress is saved to `localStorage`. There's no backend.

**This is a Svelte web app, not a MapleStory Worlds (MSW) project.**

## Stack and commands

- Svelte 5 (runes: `$state`, `$derived`, `$effect`, `$props`), TypeScript, Vite, ts-fsrs for review scheduling. No router, no UI library.
- `npm run dev`: dev server on port 5173. The browser console shows content warnings from `items.ts`.
- `npm run check`: svelte-check plus tsc. Run it after changes; it should report 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- `npm test`: Vitest, for the pure rules (`srs.test.ts`, `garden.test.ts`). CI runs it too.
- `npm run build`: production build into `dist/`, with base `/MyJapaneseGarden/` for GitHub Pages, which must match the repo name (`BASE_PATH=/` overrides it). `npm run preview` serves it at `localhost:4173/MyJapaneseGarden/`.
- `Launchers/Windows/*.bat` and `Launchers/Mac/*.command` start and stop the dev server by double-click. The start scripts install dependencies on first run, print the LAN URL for phones, and open the browser. There's no Linux equivalent yet.

## Conventions

- Keep `garden.ts` pure: no store, DOM, or `Date.now()` inside rules. Pass `now` in. Store functions in `store.svelte.ts` apply the rules and call `save()`.
- Every change to `app` must call `save()`, unless a bound input does it (`onchange={save}`).
- Sound callers check `app.muted` before calling `sfx.*`.
- Confirmations use `ConfirmSheet`, never `confirm()`, `alert()` or `prompt()`.
- Item ids come from their content (`v-<jp>-<meaning>`, `n5-<jp>-<meaning>`, `j-<kanji>`, `g-<sentence>`, `h-`/`k-<kana>`), and stats are stored under them. Changing a row's prompt or main meaning resets its stats.
- Typed answers are stored trimmed and lowercased. Grammar rows should include romaji alternatives. For vocab and kanji, `accepted` also passes an answer without its parentheses or leading "to ", so "to wear (on the body)" needs no extra row values.
- Saved data: key `genki-study-v1` (kept through the rename, so saves carry over), `SAVE_VERSION = 12`. Loaded or imported garden data always goes through `normalizeGarden`. If the save shape changes, bump the version and migrate in `repair()` (used by both `load()` and `importData()`).
- Code style: no semicolons, single quotes, 2-space indent, short comments that explain why.
- UI look: colour tokens are on `:root` in `app.css` (Honey harvest; text colours kept at 4.5:1 or better). The font is Zen Maru Gothic, self-hosted from `@fontsource/zen-maru-gothic` (unicode-range chunks, so only used characters download). Only Mochi gets a face. Main buttons are squishy pebbles (`.primary`), panels are stitched (dashed outline), sheets are soft sheets that rise with a bounce.

## Game rules (quick reference)

- 10 plots (3-2-3-2 staggered) and 10 species, two per deck. Saves from before version 7 had 16: `shrinkGarden` keeps the 10 most-grown, and `repair()` presses each extra bloom into the collection and pays `GROW_COINS × stage` for each other extra. Stages run 1 (Seeded) to 7 (Bloomed). An empty plot is `null`.
- Phases: `thirsty`, then a quiz is done, then `resting` (20 h `COOLDOWN_MS`, shown as a filling bar with a clock under the plant), then `ready`, then tap to grow. Tapping a ready plant skips the plot sheet and plays `GrowBeam`; the stage and coins only change at its peak, so a beam cut short (a quiz, leaving the screen) leaves the plant ready. A bloomed plant goes back to `thirsty` (groom) after each cooldown.
- Quiz flow: a right answer moves on after 800 ms (1500 on a combo milestone at 3, 5, 7 and 10, whose callout has a gloss to read), except the last question, a card just mastered and a kanji whose readings weren't bought (they only appear then). A wrong answer waits for Next. Cards never asked before show a New label. "I don't know" counts as a miss, without the buzzer or shake. "So close!" only follows a typed near miss (`nearMiss`: one edit, or two on answers of 9+ letters, and never on answers under 4); it's still wrong. After a watering or grooming quiz, up to `REVIEW_MAX` (5) misses are asked once more. The plant is already credited, and the review gives no XP and records no stats; Skip jumps to the summary, which lists the misses. The chapter chips are hidden during garden quizzes (`quizState`).
- XP: 10 + 2×combo (capped at 10) for each correct answer, plus 50 for watering or 30 for grooming when a quiz is finished. A level is every 1200 XP (`XP_PER_LEVEL`).
- Coins: +10 for each growth stage, +5 for each weed. Spending 100 ends a resting plant's cooldown at once (`skipWait`) and goes straight on to growing it.
- Drawing (`kind: 'draw'`): single kana and kanji (`canDraw`; not combos or harder versions), only once a card has been met, and never in weed quizzes. Answer style Mix adds it as a third kind; Drawing asks it whenever it can. The prompt is the romaji (or the kanji's meaning with its readings, free). Any character of the deck with the same main romaji passes (`drawAnswers`: じ/ぢ, ず/づ; を doesn't take "o", nor へ "e"). The stroke count must match. Strokes are compared in their own order, and a drawing closer to a look-alike of the deck (れ for わ) is wrong (`judge`). Right strokes in the wrong order count as right, but the card flags it, plays the right order and waits for Next. A stroke drawn backwards is a different stroke (ソ/ン and シ/ツ differ that way).
- Typing in Japanese: "I can type in Japanese" in Settings (`app.kana`, off by default). When on, a typed question on a vocab card already met turns around half the time (`FLIP_CHANCE`): the English is shown, the reading hidden, and the word is typed in Japanese. It's recorded against the card as usual. Harder English to Japanese cards can then be typed as well as chosen. `acceptedJapanese` takes the word as written or in kana, katakana words in hiragana, without ～, （～を） or spaces, and either side of ／.
- Kanji hints: in every quiz, a kanji's readings stay hidden until the question is answered, or until the player pays `HINT_COST` (5) coins during it. Vocab readings and grammar translations stay free.
- Weeds: one every 2 h (`WEED_EVERY_MS`), at most 6, caught up from `weedAt` on load. Weeds ask the words that need tending: the deck comes from a random one of the shakiest cards (`weedDeck`, drawn from `weakItems`, so a deck with more of them comes up more), and that deck's shakiest cards are asked first (`weedCards`), then its usual questions (`Quiz`'s `more`). Before anything's been studied, and in the tutorial (hiragana), the deck is picked at random among decks with items in the pool. Tapping one shows a pre-screen naming the question's deck (Start or ✕), then asks a single question from that deck. A correct answer pulls it for 5 coins and 5 bonus XP; a wrong one leaves it and swaps in a new question. With 2 or more weeds the pre-screen also offers Pull all: one right answer per weed from that same deck. They all come out together at the end, and quitting early keeps those already answered.
- Chapters: the chips choose which lessons' vocab, kanji and grammar are in the pool. Hiragana and katakana are always fully in it (`poolFor`). N5 Prep is vocab outside Genki (`lesson: 'n5'`). N5 Kanji (`'n5k'`) brings in the 80 N5 kanji from their Genki lessons through the item's `also` field, so turning on both a lesson and N5 Kanji never asks a kanji twice. A vocab word written as a single kanji-deck kanji is left out of vocab (`items.ts`).
- Scheduling (`srs.ts`): every recorded answer updates the card's FSRS state; right is Good, wrong is Again. A quiz asks due cards first (most overdue), then new cards in pool order up to `NEW_PER_DAY` (10 a day in each deck, counted per deck in `daily.newCards`), then the cards closest to due that weren't answered today, then new cards past the cap, and only then cards already answered today, so a plant can always be watered without the same few words coming back every quiz. A quiz doesn't repeat a card until the pool runs out. Saves from before version 10 are seeded from their right/wrong counts, with a streak of 5 landing at 32 days.
- Mastery: a card is mastered at `MASTERY_DAYS` (21) of stability, so it can drop back out after a miss, and crossing the line again celebrates again. The quiz card says so and waits, the summary lists it first, and a visitor's card stays open to say so (`record` reports the crossing). Stats shows a ring per deck over the cards in the pool. Mastering a card unlocks its harder version (`EXTRA_CARDS`, `side`): English to Japanese for vocab, readings for kanji. These are scheduled separately, always multiple choice, and come ahead of new cards (`studyPool`).
- Tend weak words: up to `TEND_LENGTH` (7) of the shakiest cards, `TEND_PER_DAY` (2) sessions a day, counted at Start. `TEND_XP` (5) per right answer, with no combo, plant, streak or review round. The summary tells the player to take a break: this must not become cramming.
- Seed packets: one per level past the first (`seedPackets`, derived from XP and `seedsUsed`). Planting with a packet lets the player choose the bloom colour, or keep the packet with Surprise me.
- Rolls favour the new: blooms and visitors not yet found are `FAVOUR_NEW` (2) times as likely as found ones.
- Garden wording: "You can read N words" counts vocab with `KNOWN_DAYS` (7) of stability, and the summary marks new ones and milestones. Ready plants are named first in the status, since growing one needs no quiz. Once today's study is done, the status says one plant is enough, and a link offers Tend weak words while there are shaky words and sessions left; the summary links there too when something was missed.
- Ants: a line of 1-3 crosses the lawn every 15-40 s while the garden is open, some carrying crumbs or food. Once `ANT_COIN_EVERY_MS` (1 h) has passed since the last ant coin was collected (`antCoinAt`), the next lead ant carries a coin; tapping it gives 5 coins. An untapped coin ant leaves the coin for a later ant. With reduced motion, only the coin ant shows, standing still.
- A deck's plants unlock once it has at least `MIN_DECK_SIZE` (10) items.
- Perfect quiz: 10/10 on a watering or grooming quiz gives +15 coins (`PERFECT_COINS`). The summary also shows the best combo of the run.
- Streak (`streak.ts`): days in a row with a finished watering or grooming quiz, counted at local midnight. Every 7th day earns a rain day (at most 2), and each covers one missed day. A streak that can't be covered restarts at day 1, with no other penalty. Milestone coins: 15 at day 3, 30 at day 7, 50 at day 14, and 100 every 30 days.
- Collection: a species/variant is discovered when a plant of it blooms. Once a bloomed plant has been groomed `PRESERVE_GROOMS` (3) times, it can be preserved, after a confirmation: the plot is freed, the flower is pressed into the collection (completing it), and after the `PressFlower` scene the player is taken to the Collection page. It pays 25 coins, plus 50 the first time a flower is completed.
- Welcome back: opening the garden after 4 h or more away shows what changed (ready and thirsty plants, new weeds, an ant coin, the streak or rain).
- Shop and decorations: 20 decorations (30 to 800 coins) can be bought any number of times and placed on the open lawn spots (`DECOR_SPOTS`, kept clear of weed spots). 6 are open from the start; 6 more cost 150 to 1000 coins each (`SPOT_PRICES`). Saved as `decor: { owned, placed, tiers, open }`, repaired by `normalizeDecor`. If the shop was opened from an empty spot's picker, the next purchase is placed on that spot.
- Upgrades: per kind, so every copy changes together. Plain to Adorned costs 2× the price and adds a touch unique to that kind; Adorned to Golden costs 4× and turns it gold. Upgrades are cosmetic. The gift rock prices as 50.
- Garden looks: 4 lawns, 3 soils, 3 bed edgings and 4 atmospheres on top of the free originals (200 to 900 coins). Bought once, swapped free, and tried on in the shop's preview before paying. Saved as `look: { owned, wearing }`, repaired by `normalizeLook`.
- Shiny rock: a gift-only decoration from the tutorial (`gift: true`, so it's left out of `SHOP_DECOR` and `buyDecor` refuses it). `giftRock` gives it once, even on a replay.
- Tutorial: new saves start at `tour: 'hello'`; saves from before version 6 skip it. Steps: welcome, the starter plant, plant (the sheet closes without offering water), HUD, visitor, ant, weed, shop (the first + tapped holds a gift: a shiny rock, placed on that spot, then a button to the shop), inside the shop, tap the 💧, take the quiz. During it, visitors and coin ants only come on their own steps (called in at once; the coin ant stops halfway along its lane until tapped), and the weed asks hiragana. `prepTour` makes sure each step's target exists. Closing the plot sheet on the last step goes back to the 💧 step. Replay from the menu or Stats; skip from the card.
- Starter plant: the tutorial's own species (`STARTER`), left out of `GROWABLE`, so it's never in the picker or the collection. It starts one growth from blooming. Its 💧 opens the guided あ question (`StarterQuiz`, not recorded against the card, +12 XP), watering it skips the rest, ✨ plays the usual `GrowBeam` (+10 coins), and ✂️ plays `GroomSnip` with no quiz, after which it goes to seed and frees its plot for the plant step. `repair()` and `setTour` clear it whenever the tour isn't on a starter step, and a replay with no free plot skips those steps.
- Level-ups: a popup celebrates each new level (it plays the level-up sound; the quiz no longer does). It waits while a quiz holds `levelHold` (`holdLevelUps()`): a garden or weed quiz until it closes, free practice until Next is pressed.
- Visitors: once `VISITOR_EVERY_MS` (20 min) has passed since the last one was thanked, a creature comes, with an even chance of each creature and then of each of its looks (`rollCreature`). Butterflies, dragonflies, bees and ladybugs usually land on a flower; birds and owls swoop to the lawn; frogs, rabbits, cats and tanuki hop in from the side. It carries a rolled-up note until thanked. Tapping it shows a vocab word with its meaning hidden (missed words come up more often). The player reveals it and answers "How did you do?" with Got it! or Still learning, which is recorded like a quiz answer, and either gives 1-3 coins and befriends the creature. Closing the card records nothing. Each finished watering or grooming quiz also brings a visitor, `QUIZ_VISITORS` (3) times a day. One that isn't thanked leaves after 2.5 min, and a later one comes instead.
- Creature collection: answering a visitor's note logs that creature and look in `creatures` (`meetCreature`), unlocking it on the Collection's Creatures tab. Closing the card without answering doesn't. Keys use look ids, so looks can be cut without shifting saves; `normalizeCreatures` drops entries for cut looks. There's no coin reward for a new one.
